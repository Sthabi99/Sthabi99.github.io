CAPSTONE PROJECT / INDEPENDENT BUILD

Kasi-Till

A till that fits in your pocket.

A mobile-first point-of-sale and stock-management system for South African spaza shops. Designed around affordable Android phones, power interruptions and intermittent connectivity.

I built the Android app, backend APIs and owner portal, with local storage, queued sales synchronisation, staff access and reporting. The backend uses PostgreSQL and Flyway, with a Docker-based AWS deployment.
