YOUR FIVE-DAY OUTLOOK

Built with JavaScript, HTML and CSS. Change cities, switch units or generate another outlook.

How it works

Choose a city, generate five days of example weather and switch between Celsius and Fahrenheit.

JavaScript creates five daily objects with random weather values and displays them as cards. Changing units converts the stored temperatures; changing cities generates a new set. The values come from the script, not a weather service.

Concepts: Arrays, objects, dates, random numbers and DOM rendering.
