How it works

Set your stock, selling price and cost per glass, then run five days of trading.

For each day, the script estimates demand from the generated temperature and selling price, limits sales to remaining stock and records the result. Profit is revenue minus the cost of every glass prepared, including unsold stock.

Concepts: Loops, arrays, stock tracking and profit calculations.
